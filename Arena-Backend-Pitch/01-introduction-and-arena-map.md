## How to answer any experience question: **SCOPE**

- **S - Situation:** what business problem existed?
- **C - Contribution:** what did you personally do?
- **O - Operation:** how did the request flow technically?
- **P - Protection:** validation, transaction, authorization, logging, tests.
- **E - Effect:** what became safer, faster, or easier?

Example:

> A driver document-expiry workflow needed reliable reminders. I worked on the notification logic and attachment query. The background process selected eligible driver documents, applied expiry rules, built the message, and sent a push notification. I added structured logs and handled missing data so one invalid record would not make the whole workflow fail. This made reminders more reliable and easier to troubleshoot.

## Rules that preserve confidence

- Say **“I implemented”** only for work you personally implemented.
- Say **“I contributed to”** for shared work.
- Say **“the project uses”** when the technology exists but you did not own it.
- If you do not know: **“I have not implemented that directly, but my understanding is...”**
- Never defend a wrong resume entry. Correct it briefly and move forward.
